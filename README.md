## Why Use Xnov?

Most free online image compressors and background removers either force you to upload files or require an account. Xnov processes everything in the browser, so your images and videos never leave your device. This makes it useful for:

- Compressing photos before sending them
- Removing EXIF metadata for privacy
- Changing ID photo backgrounds
- Quick background removal without third-party servers
- Basic video trimming and compression

Supported image formats include JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, and BMP. Video formats include MP4, WEBM, MOV, and MKV. Batch processing is supported on most tools (download results as ZIP).
