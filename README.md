# Xnov imgtool— Free Image & Video Tools

Free online image and video tools that run entirely in your browser.  
No uploads, no signup, no watermarks, no usage limits.

**Try it here:** https://xnov.io

Everything is processed locally on your device. Drop a file in, get the result out.

## Image Tools (16)

- **Image Merger** — Combine multiple images into one (vertical or horizontal)
- **Resize Image** — Scale by pixels or percentage, optional aspect-ratio lock
- **Rotate Image** — Rotate or flip any direction
- **Meme Maker** — Type text with live preview
- **Remove Background** — AI background removal, transparent PNG, fully local
- **Crop Image** — Free crop or common aspect ratios
- **Image Enhance** — Real-ESRGAN 2×/4× upscaler that restores detail without over-processing
- **Split Image** — Slice into grid tiles and download as ZIP
- **Add Watermark** — Text watermark with 9-grid positioning and free dragging
- **Convert to JPG** — PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP → JPG
- **Blur / Redact** — Blur or mosaic faces, plates, or any area
- **Watermark Remover** — Select a watermark and rebuild the area from surrounding pixels
- **Compress Image** — Shrink JPG/PNG/WEBP while keeping quality
- **Convert from JPG** — JPG → PNG or WEBP
- **ID Photo Background** — One-click background swap (white, blue, red, etc.)
- **EXIF Cleaner** — Strip GPS, camera info, timestamps and other metadata

## Video Tools (3)

- **Trim Video** — Cut with second-level precision
- **Compress Video** — Reduce file size with resolution and quality presets
- **Video to GIF** — Convert a segment to animated GIF with custom FPS and width

## Why this exists

Most online image tools either force you to upload your files, require an account, or add watermarks. This one does none of that. It’s just a collection of focused tools that work the moment you open the page.

Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP for images; MP4, WEBM, MOV, MKV for video. Batch processing is supported (download as ZIP).

---

Not trying to be Photoshop. Just practical tools that keep your files on your machine.
